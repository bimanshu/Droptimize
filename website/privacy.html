Privacy

Your files never leave your Mac.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Droptimize makes every file on your Mac. It never uploads your files, has no analytics and needs no account. It goes online for two small things only: checking a Pro license key with Dodo Payments, and checking for updates on GitHub.

Your files

Everything Droptimize does to an image, video, PDF or Word document (converting, compressing, resizing, removing the background, muting) happens on your Mac. Your files, their contents and their names are never sent anywhere. Removing a background uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Turning a Word document into a PDF uses Word or Pages on your Mac if you have them, and Droptimize's own reader if you don't.

The History window keeps a list of the files you made (names, sizes, dates and a link to where they are) in a file on your Mac. It never leaves your Mac, and Clear History deletes it.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • Pro license (only if you buy Pro). When you activate a key, the app sends the key and your Mac's name (so you can tell your Macs apart) to Dodo Payments. About once a week it asks Dodo whether the key is still valid, so a refunded key stops working. Nothing about your files is sent.
  • Update check. About 30 seconds after launch and then once a day, the app downloads a small file from GitHub that says which version is newest. It sends nothing about you. An update is only downloaded when you click Install.

Like any request on the internet, these reach Dodo Payments and GitHub with your IP address, which they handle under their own privacy policies. The free plan works without ever going online.

Buying Pro

Pro is sold by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record: they take the payment, handle tax and invoices, and send you the license key. Your card details go to Dodo, never to us. Dodo shares the order with us (your name, email address, country and what you bought) so we can help you with your purchase. See Dodo Payments' privacy policy.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts are served from here. The company hosting this site, and GitHub, where the app is downloaded from, may log visits (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to reply, and keep it only as long as the conversation needs.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you (at most, your order details from Dodo and any emails), and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to support@droptimize.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever needs to send anything new, it will say so here first.
